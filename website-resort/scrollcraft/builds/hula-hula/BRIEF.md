# Hula Hula. A playful tropical escape

Self-authored under the user's creative direction: “make multilple layers when you scroll and make it fun and creative but still good layout and good functionality across”. This extends the existing React resort website and preserves its working controls.

## Evidence and decisions

1. Vibe: the user requests fun, creative, layered motion. Authored direction: a bright tropical holiday scrapbook.
2. Journey: retain room discovery, food, activities, photos, celebrations, and booking. Add the specifically requested fruit cart and shuttle photographs.
3. Energy: a welcoming dimensional hero, calm room browsing, one joyful breakfast peak, playful food and transport, practical booking.
4. Memorable moment: the user says “also i love the flooting breakfast”. A breakfast postcard floats over a pool photograph as the visitor scrolls.
5. Signature move: the Breakfast Float. A pool-photo plane, floating breakfast postcard, ripple rings, and semantic headline settle at different rates into a stable breakfast scene.
6. Range: playful, photographic, logo-led. Retain the existing Fredoka/DM Sans pairing.
7. Distinct scenes with native vertical scrolling and anchored navigation. No scroll hijacking. Short sticky moment on wide screens; normal document flow on phones and with motion disabled.
8. Supplied assets: real resort photos, fruit-cart and shuttle promotions, floating breakfasts, and the original logo. No generated photos are needed.

## Brand and layer contract

Colors come from the logo: leaf green, turquoise water, cyan sky, pineapple yellow, their darker shades, and their white tints. The original SVG remains intact and is displayed larger.

Hero: pale botanical backdrop (far), resort photograph (midground), breakfast postcard (foreground), independent sun/sticker plane, stable headline and booking controls. No extracted people or moving contact points.

Breakfast: genuine pool scene behind a real breakfast photograph in a separate physical postcard. Ripple illustrations are decorative and never conceal text. Opening: complete scene and headline. Midpoint: postcard gently approaches and tilts while ripples expand. Exit: all content remains legible and flows into dining.

The peak is: “Their floating breakfast came to life as I moved through the page.” Tell-someone sentence: “It's the site where breakfast floats into your holiday.” There is no authored empty scroll.

Grammar: a custom holiday scrapbook. Anchored navigation, overlapping photographic prints, one short sticky photo peak, manual room rails, a practical booking close. No video scrubbing, continuous-world travel, unreadable overlays, or pinned text arguments. The existing eight grammars each impose a structure unsuitable for preserving this booking website, so this grammar explicitly combines a photo journey with practical navigation.

Fingerprint gate: first build in this project's registry. No historical rows to compare.
