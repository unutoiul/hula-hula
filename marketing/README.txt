DIMENSIONS
Cards: 1402 × 1122 px, approximately 5:4 landscape.
Mascot: 1254 × 1254 px with transparency.
Keep each image's proportions when resizing.

PREVIEW
Open preview-gallery.html in a browser. The gallery uses only the included local images.
Transparent areas are shown over a checkerboard in the gallery; that checkerboard is not in the PNG.

STYLE
Warm ivory frame, rounded artwork corners, bold green / teal serif titles,
hand-painted gouache and print texture, tropical foliage, coral hibiscus,
turquoise water and the familiar pineapple in sunglasses.

These are creative illustrations and activity concepts, rather than photographs or a confirmed description of the club's facilities.

generation-prompts.json contains the final prompts and reference files.
